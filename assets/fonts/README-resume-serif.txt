Resume Serif (resume-serif-regular/bold/italic.woff2)

These are modified versions of Latin Modern Roman 10 (LMRoman10-Regular, -Bold and -Italic,
version 2.004), copyright 2003, 2009 B. Jackowski and J. M. Nowacki (on behalf of TeX users groups).
They are used by the resume page (/resume/) so that it matches the LaTeX PDF resume.

Changes: cut down to Latin letters and common punctuation, renamed "Resume Serif" (as the GUST Font
License requests for derived works) and converted to WOFF2 by scripts/resume_fonts.py in the site's
repository, which also explains how to rebuild them from the originals.

The originals are distributed under the GUST Font License (the LaTeX Project Public License 1.3c with
a renaming request): http://www.gust.org.pl/fonts/licenses/GUST-FONT-LICENSE.txt
Original fonts: https://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/latin-modern and https://ctan.org/pkg/lm
