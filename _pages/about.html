Girish Kulathumani

I’m a machine learning researcher working on EEG and EMG decoding. I graduated in Computer Science and Engineering from VIT Chennai in 2025, spent a year as an application engineer at Accenture, and have two peer-reviewed papers.

Open to research engineer, research assistant and PhD roles in biosignal ML. Available now · based in Chennai, open to relocation and remote.

Research focus

  • Cross-subject biosignal decoding — EEG and wrist surface EMG models that work on new people with little or no calibration. I’m currently an independent entrant in the NeurIPS 2026 EEG/EMG Foundation Challenge (Track 2, EEG decoding).
  • Federated learning — personalising shared models without pooling each person’s data. At VIT Chennai I led HabitatFL, a federated audio and image classifier built with Flower and Docker on the university’s NVIDIA DGX.
  • Leakage-free evaluation — subject-wise splits that rule out data leakage.
